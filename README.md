# Vein_Detection

Visualizing the veins is a major problem faced by phlebotomists while
performing IV access or extract blood from the veins. As the veins are
located below 3mm below the surface of the skin, people with fair skin,
obese and according to different age groups locating the veins becomes more
complex. Therefore, doctors or practitioners may be needed to prick a
number of times to locate the right veins and then perform the IV access.
This is painful as the number of pricks increases the tissue gets bumped and
this issue is not taken serious by many people. There are several vein finder
products exists, but they only visualize and project the veins over the skin of
the patient. To address this problem a vein finder device has been proposed.
This will identify the location of the veins but also measures their size. In
addition, it recommends the appropriate needle gauge size based on the
detected dimensions of the vein. The device has been successfully tested on
people of different ages, 5-70 years, with different body conditions. A CNN
based approach is applied to ensure that the captured images contain veins
before processing. One of the notable achievements of this device is its
remarkable efficiency, as the vein viewing process takes less than 1ms. By
combining recommendations for vein position, size and needle gauge
recommendations, this solution significantly improves the accuracy and
speed up the process of venipuncture by reducing patient discomfort and the
need for multiple needle pricks.
